    01

    Scope

    This policy covers:

    • Shopify applications listed in the Shopify App Store or installed on merchant stores
    • Web applications hosted at reactwithcode.com or related domains
    • Mobile applications distributed via the Apple App Store and/or Google Play

    Some Services store data only temporarily to process a request ("pass-through"), while others store data on our servers to provide ongoing functionality (e.g., saved settings, analytics, logs). The handling depends on the specific Service — see Section 4.

    02

    Information we collect

    Account & store information

    • Shop name, domain, email, and plan details (for Shopify apps, via the Shopify Admin API)
    • Account credentials or tokens required to authenticate API access

    Customer / end-user data (where applicable)

    • Order, product, or customer information accessed via granted API scopes, solely to provide the app's functionality
    • Contact details submitted through forms, checkout extensions, or app features

    Usage & technical data

    • Log data (IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited)
    • Analytics events (feature usage, error logs) used to improve the Services
    • Cookies or similar technologies on web applications

    Mobile-specific data

    • Device identifiers, push notification tokens, and permissions you grant (camera, location, etc.), only where required for a specific feature

    We only collect what is necessary for the Service in question to function.

    03

    How we use information

    • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service
    • Authenticate and process API requests (e.g., Shopify Admin/Storefront API calls)
    • Diagnose issues, monitor performance, and improve functionality
    • Communicate with you about updates, support, or billing
    • Comply with legal obligations

    We do not sell personal data. We do not use merchant or customer data for advertising purposes.

    04

    Data handling by service type

    Service typeData storage approach
    Shopify apps (pass-through) Data is processed in-memory or transiently to fulfill a request and is not persisted on our servers beyond what's needed for the transaction.
    Shopify apps (persistent) App settings, cached store data, or logs may be stored in our database (e.g., hosting provider) to support ongoing features.
    Web applications User-submitted data and analytics may be stored in our database to provide account features and improve the product.
    Mobile applications Device tokens and user-generated content may be stored to support notifications, sync, or saved preferences.

    Update this table per app if a specific product needs more detail — you can link out to an app-specific addendum if one product differs meaningfully from the rest.

    We may share data with trusted service providers who help us operate the Services, such as:

    • Hosting/infrastructure providers (e.g., Vercel / Render / AWS)
    • Analytics providers (e.g., Google Analytics / Plausible)

    • Payment processors (e.g., Shopify Billing API, Stripe)

    These providers are contractually obligated to protect data and use it only to perform services on our behalf. We do not share data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

    We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the Service or comply with legal requirements. Merchants and users may request deletion of their data by contacting us (Section 11).

    For Shopify apps, we comply with Shopify's mandatory GDPR webhooks — customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact — to handle data subject requests automatically.
